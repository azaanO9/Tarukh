# Outpost asset attribution

These notices cover `sources/*.glb` and the third-party content embedded in both assembled outpost GLBs. The individual licenses below remain applicable; the assembled scene does not replace them with a single blanket license. Retain these notices and the source manifest when redistributing the models.

The GLB binaries are kept locally and excluded from the current GitHub tree. These notices remain for the review previews and local models; the source links below identify the original providers.

## Original TARUKH components

`sources/architecture.glb` contains the TARUKH shell, partitions, floor, labels, entries and roof from the earlier review asset, with its handmade furniture removed. `sources/surface-ops.svg` and its PNG are original display artwork. The assembly and render setup are in `build_outpost.py`.

The supplied Mars Dune Alpha floor plan is a layout reference, not a redistributed texture or official 3D model. Mars Dune Alpha is an Earth-based CHAPEA analog habitat, with architectural attribution to ICON/BIG. See [NASA CHAPEA](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/chapea/) and [BIG](https://big.dk/projects/mars-chapea-4310). Heights, equipment and roof treatment are artistic interpretations. No NASA endorsement or construction certification is implied.

The source manifest records exact download URLs and SHA-256 hashes. Assembly checks those hashes before importing any furniture.
